# Jacobs Coffee Sales Dashboard

An interactive, single-page dashboard analysing 2025 coffee sales for the Jacobs brand across Europe.

**Live demo:** <add your GitHub Pages link here>

## Overview
- 65 products in 9 categories (Whole Beans, Filter Coffee, Espresso Capsules, TASSIMO, etc.)
- 8 markets: Germany, Austria, Switzerland, Netherlands, Belgium, France, Poland, Czech Republic
- 5 channels: Supermarket, Drugstore, Online, Convenience, Foodservice
- 2,600 sales records: €16.16M revenue, 2.93M units

## Dashboard features
- KPI cards: revenue, units, average price per unit, product count
- Filters for market, channel, category and pack size (all charts update together)
- Revenue by category, market, channel and pack size
- Average price per unit by category
- Monthly revenue and units
- Top 10 products
- Market × channel revenue heatmap
- Light and dark mode support

## Key findings
- TASSIMO is the largest category (26.5% of revenue), followed by Whole Beans (16.5%) and Filter Coffee (12.6%).
- Switzerland generates 19.4% of revenue from 12.7% of units, with an average price of €8.39 versus about €4.4 in Poland and Czechia.
- Foodservice is the top channel (23.5%) and ranks first in all 8 markets.
- Whole Beans have the highest price per unit (€9.51); Coffee Sticks the lowest (€2.79).
- Flavored products account for 24.7% of revenue; decaf only 2.8%.
- Intensity 5 and 6 products generate about half of revenue among intensity-rated lines.

### Data notes
- No missing values or duplicates in the fact table; revenue = units × unit price on every row.
- The fact table has exactly one row per product × market × channel combination (65 × 8 × 5), each with a single month and pack size. Monthly and pack-size views should be read as a sample, not a true time series.
